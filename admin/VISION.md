## Current Vision

### Project: Predicting Future Amazon Purchases Using Demographics and Purchase Histories

**Primary Stakeholder:** Amazon Customers who prioritize convenience and searchability when utilizing e-commerce platforms

**Secondary Stakeholders:**:
- Amazon executives who wish to increase sales and facilitate positive perceptions of the Amazon platform
- Sellers who want to identify likely buyers and promote their products appropriately


**Problem Statement:** Amazon’s product recommendation model uses purchase histories and overall customer behavior to create personalized recommendations, but fails to account for the impact of demographic factors that may influence purchase behavior. 

**Solution:** Build a comprehensive product recommendation system that uses a combination of purchase histories and demographic factors, in addition to overall consumer purchase behavior, to predict future purchases and create appropriate recommendations.
